# Resume Project Entry

## Recommended version

**ESP32 Urban Air Pollution Exposure Chamber Controller | Embedded Systems Project**

- Engineered and integrated the electronics subsystem for an interdisciplinary M.Pharm air-pollution study, connecting an ESP32, PMS5003 particulate sensor, SHT31 environmental sensor, DS3231 RTC, LCD, microSD storage, MOSFET driver, and centrifugal blower.
- Developed embedded firmware to monitor PM1.0, PM2.5, PM10, temperature, and humidity; validate sensor frames; display live readings; and record timestamped CSV measurements at two-second intervals.
- Implemented PWM-based airflow control, external-load isolation, common grounding, fuse/protection considerations, and chamber-to-chamber airflow connections before handing the working prototype to the researcher for experimental testing.

## One-line version

Built an ESP32-based particulate exposure-chamber controller with PM1.0/PM2.5/PM10 monitoring, temperature/humidity sensing, PWM blower control, LCD feedback, and timestamped microSD logging for a collaborator-led M.Pharm study.

## Interview explanation

I supported an M.Pharm research project investigating urban particulate exposure. My responsibility was the engineering subsystem, not the animal or biological testing. I integrated the sensors and ESP32, completed the wiring and chamber connections, controlled the external blower through a MOSFET, added electrical safeguards, and developed the data-logging firmware. After confirming that the integrated prototype operated, I handed it over to the researcher, who conducted the experimental testing and biological analysis.
